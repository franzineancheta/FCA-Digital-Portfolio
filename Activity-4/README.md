## ♡ PRESENTATION OVERVIEW

For this group activity, we explored **Google Drive** and its importance in storing, organizing, accessing, and sharing digital files.

Our presentation discussed its different features and how it allows users to collaborate and work together online. We also highlighted how Google Drive makes file management and teamwork more convenient and accessible.

### ₊˚⊹♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED? ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

Through this activity, I learned more about the importance and usefulness of cloud-based storage, particularly Google Drive, in academic and professional settings.

- I gained a better understanding of Google Drive's features, including file storage, sharing, organization, and real-time collaboration.
