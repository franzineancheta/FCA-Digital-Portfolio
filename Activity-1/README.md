## ♡ ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

For my first activity, **Presentation Design Principles**, I created a promotional poster featuring **Free Coffee at Arrupe Hall in Ateneo de Davao University**.

The goal was to create a **warm, inviting, and visually appealing poster** that would catch students' attention while clearly communicating the event information. I applied different design principles to organize the elements and make the poster both attractive and easy to understand.

<div align="center">

### ₊˚⊹♡ DESIGN PRINCIPLES APPLIED ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

#### ୨ৎ Visual Hierarchy

I made **"Free Coffee"** the largest and most noticeable element. This allows the audience to immediately understand the main message of the poster.

#### ୨ৎ Balance and Alignment

I placed the university logo at the top, event details in the middle, and coffee-related illustrations around the layout to create a balanced composition.

#### ୨ৎ Contrast

I used a light cream background with darker brown elements, allowing the text and illustrations to stand out.

#### ୨ৎ Consistency and Repetition

I used the same warm brown color and repeated coffee-related elements throughout the design to create a consistent visual style.

<div align="center">

### ₊˚⊹♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED? ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

Through this activity, I learned that good presentation design is not only about making something attractive but also about **organizing visual elements so that the audience can easily understand the message**.

- I learned how to apply design principles such as visual hierarchy, balance, alignment, contrast, and consistency when creating a promotional poster.
- I also gained a better understanding of how colors, fonts, and illustrations work together to create a specific mood and attract the audience's attention.
- One challenge I encountered was arranging the elements without making the poster look too crowded. I addressed this by adjusting the placement, size, and spacing of the text and illustrations.
- This activity helped me become more mindful of the importance of readability and proper organization in designing visual materials.
- In the future, I can apply these skills when creating presentations, infographics, and other digital materials. I also hope to improve my creativity and explore more design techniques to make my outputs more effective and visually engaging.
