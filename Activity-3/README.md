## ♡ ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

For my third activity, I created a **social media infographic about vaccine hesitancy**.

The goal was to present important information about vaccine hesitancy in a way that is **simple, informative, and easy to understand**, especially for people viewing it on social media. Through this activity, I aimed to raise awareness and communicate health-related information using visual elements and concise content.

<div align="center">

### ₊˚⊹♡ DESIGN APPROACH ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

I first identified the important information I wanted to communicate. Then, I organized the content into sections and used different design elements to make the infographic clear and engaging.

The elements I focused on included:

- 🎨 **Visual Elements** – Used illustrations and graphics to support the information and make the infographic more engaging.
- 🔤 **Typography** – Selected readable fonts and appropriate text sizes to emphasize important details.
- 🌈 **Colors** – Used suitable color combinations to create a visually appealing and organized design.
- 📐 **Layout** – Arranged the information into sections to maintain proper flow and organization.
- 📝 **Concise Information** – Focused on the most important points to avoid overwhelming the audience with too much text.

Since infographics are meant to communicate information quickly, I made sure that the content was easy to follow and that the key messages were noticeable.

### ₊˚⊹♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED? ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

Through this activity, I learned that **visual design is not only used to make content attractive but can also serve as an effective tool for education and awareness**. It helped me understand how information can be presented in a way that captures attention while making important messages easier to understand.

- I learned how to organize health-related information into short, meaningful sections without losing the main message.
- I improved my skills in combining visual elements, typography, colors, and layout to create an informative and engaging infographic.
- One challenge I encountered was deciding which information to include because the topic contained several important details. I addressed this by selecting the most relevant points and simplifying the explanations while keeping the main message clear.
- This activity helped me realize the importance of presenting accurate and understandable information, especially when discussing health-related topics.
- In the future, I can apply these skills when creating health education materials, awareness campaigns, and informational posts. As a nursing student, I can also use visual communication to help patients and communities better understand health information.
