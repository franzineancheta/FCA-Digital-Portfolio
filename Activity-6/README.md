### Personal Website Development – Get to Know Me

### ୨ৎ Activity Description

This activity involved creating a personalized, interactive, and visually appealing website using Replit. The website, titled **"Created by Chin – Get to Know Me,"** presents my journey from childhood to the present through personal memories, hobbies, interests, favorite things, pets, and future aspirations.

Designed as a digital scrapbook, the website allows visitors to learn more about my personality and the little things that make me who I am. It also gave me an opportunity to explore website design and development while expressing my creativity.

### ୨ৎ Design Approach and Principles

The website follows a soft, feminine, nostalgic, and scrapbook-inspired aesthetic. I used the following design elements:

- **Color Palette:** Cream (#FBF8ED), pastel pink (#E9AACA), lime green (#C5CD55), and rose pink (#D875A3) to create a cohesive and playful appearance.
- **Typography:** Pony Club, Aprila, and The Youngest served as the primary font inspirations, with similar alternatives where needed.
- **Layout:** Scrapbook-inspired sections, polaroid-style photo frames, rounded cards, and organized content.
- **Visual Elements:** Delicate flowers, hearts, stars, bows, and decorative details to make the website more personal.
- **Navigation:** A single-page layout with navigation links that guide visitors through the different sections of the website.

The website includes sections such as Home, My Story So Far, The Little Things About Me, My Favorite Things, My Pets, Me Lately, and Contact.

### ୨ৎ What Have I Learned?

- **Website Design:** I learned how to organize different sections and create a visually appealing website with a consistent design.
- **Creativity and Personalization:** I discovered how colors, typography, images, and layouts can communicate personality and make a website more meaningful.
- **Technical Skills:** I gained experience using Replit and learned more about website development, including basic HTML, CSS, and responsive design concepts.
- **Challenges and Improvements:** Arranging the elements and achieving my desired design required several adjustments. I learned to be more patient and attentive to detail throughout the process.
- **Future Application:** I can apply these skills when creating personal portfolios, digital presentations, and other creative websites, as well as developing my digital skills for future projects.
