### AI-Assisted Resume Creation and Comparison

### ୨ৎ Activity Description

This activity involved using AI tools, specifically ChatGPT and Gemini, to create a professional one-page resume based on my personal information, educational background, and clinical experiences. Both tools were given the same instructions to produce a formal and organized resume using dark navy blue and black colors.

After generating the resumes, I compared their strengths and weaknesses based on their structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality to determine which output was more suitable for a professional nursing application.

### ୨ৎ Design Approach and Principles

The main goal was to create a formal, concise, and professional resume suitable for a future nursing position. The following elements were considered:

- **Color Palette:** Dark navy blue and black were used to create a professional and formal appearance.
- **Layout:** Organized sections were used to make the information easy to read and navigate.
- **Typography:** Clear and readable fonts were preferred to maintain a clean and professional presentation.
- **Content Organization:** Clinical experiences, educational background, and seminars attended were arranged into separate sections.
- **AI Tool Comparison:** ChatGPT and Gemini outputs were evaluated based on their presentation, relevance, completeness, and readability.

### ୨ৎ Comparison of AI-Generated Resumes

**ChatGPT**

ChatGPT produced a clean, organized, and professional one-page resume. Its strengths included concise descriptions, readable formatting, and a formal navy blue and black color scheme. However, it lacked a professional summary and a separate skills section, and it provided limited details about specific clinical assignments.

**Gemini**

Gemini created a well-organized resume with a professional healthcare-inspired design. It highlighted clinical responsibilities and leadership roles, such as Staff Nurse, Charge Nurse, and Head Nurse. However, it included unnecessary early educational details, lacked quantifiable clinical information, and retained generic placeholders that required editing.

**Overall Comparison**

Both tools produced useful resume drafts with different strengths. ChatGPT was more concise and straightforward, while Gemini provided more specific descriptions of clinical responsibilities. Both outputs still required personal review and revisions to ensure accuracy, relevance, and completeness.

### ୨ৎ What Have I Learned?

- **AI-Assisted Writing:** I learned how AI tools can make resume creation easier and more efficient by organizing information and improving its presentation.
- **Content Organization:** I realized the importance of presenting qualifications, educational background, and clinical experiences clearly.
- **Critical Evaluation:** Comparing ChatGPT and Gemini helped me identify the strengths and weaknesses of different AI-generated outputs.
- **Challenges and Improvements:** I learned that AI-generated resumes may contain unnecessary information or missing details, so reviewing and editing the content is important.
- **Future Application:** I can use these skills when preparing professional documents and applying for nursing positions. I also learned to use AI as a helpful tool while still applying my own judgment.
