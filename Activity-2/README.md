## ♡ ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

For my second activity, **Color Palette and Typography**, my main concept was to **showcase who I am through my choice of colors and fonts**.

The activity focused on exploring how different color combinations and typefaces can express personality, creativity, and visual identity. I experimented with different shades and font styles to create a design that represents my personal taste and aesthetic.

<div align="center">

### ₊˚⊹♡ MY COLOR PALETTE ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

I chose three colors:

<div align="center">

| Color | Hex Code |
|---|---|
| 🌸 Light Pink | `#E9AACA` |
| 🍋 Lime Green | `#C5CD55` |
| 💗 Muted Pink | `#D875A3` |

</div>

I chose these colors because they represent a **soft, playful, youthful, and creative** side of my personality. Combining these shades allowed me to create a color palette that reflects my personal style while maintaining visual harmony.

<div align="center">

### ₊˚⊹♡ TYPOGRAPHY ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

For my typography, I explored three different fonts:

- **Pony Club**
- **Aprila**
- **The Youngest**

I chose these fonts because their unique and decorative styles allow me to express **creativity and personality**.

I also experimented with **uppercase letters, lowercase letters, and numbers** to observe how each font looks and how its style changes depending on its use.

---

<div align="center">

### ₊˚⊹♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED? ♡⊹˚₊

</div>

Through this activity, I learned that **colors and typography play an important role in creating a visual identity**. Design choices can communicate emotions, personality, and creativity even without using words to explain them.

- I learned how to select and combine colors using hex codes to create a cohesive and visually appealing color palette.
- I gained a better understanding of typography, including how different font styles, sizes, and letter arrangements can influence the overall appearance of a design.
- One challenge I encountered was choosing colors and fonts that would complement each other without making the design look too overwhelming. I addressed this by experimenting with different combinations until I found a balance that matched my preferred aesthetic.
- This activity helped me become more aware of how small design choices can affect the overall presentation and impression of a visual output.
- In the future, I can apply these skills when creating digital portfolios, presentations, posters, and other creative projects. I also want to explore more font combinations and color theories to further improve my design skills.
